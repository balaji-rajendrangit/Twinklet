Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short: Twinklet does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Twinklet app for iPhone ("the app") handles information. The app is made by Balaji Rajendran ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or measurement tools, and no third party software that collects data. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves a small file on your iPhone containing:

  • the levels you have solved and your rating for each,
  • your nightly puzzle results and streak,
  • an unfinished board, so you can continue where you left off,
  • your settings, such as sound, music and colour patterns.

This file never leaves your device through the app and is never sent to us. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under Apple's terms. You can erase it at any time with Settings, Reset progress or New Game in the app, or by deleting the app.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics or crash reports with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated information such as crash reports and download counts. This does not identify you and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, notifications or any other permission.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to reply to you. We do not add it to any mailing list or share it. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that involve data, such as Game Center, iCloud sync or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@opulentdominion.in
