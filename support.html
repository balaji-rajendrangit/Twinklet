Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email support@opulentdominion.in. Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version and the version of Twinklet (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app).

How do I play?

Place one star in every row, every column and every colour area. Stars never touch, not even at the corners. Tap a square once for a dot (your note that no star goes there), tap the dot to place a star, and drag across squares to add many dots. The full illustrated guide is in the app under How to Play.

Do I ever need to guess?

No. Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can be solved one sure step at a time. If you are stuck, tap Hint: it shows the next step and explains why.

Why did I lose a rating star?

Each level starts with 5 rating stars. A wrong star or a hint costs one, but you always keep at least one and never lose the level. In the first 3 tutorial levels mistakes are free.

How is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your iPhone. Continue takes you back to the exact board you left. Progress is not stored online, so it does not move to a new iPhone by itself; it is included when you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

How do I start again?

Choose New Game on the title screen, or Settings, Reset progress. Both erase your solved levels, stars and streak after asking you to confirm. Your settings are kept.

Can I turn off the music or sounds?

Yes. Use the music note and speaker buttons at the top of the title screen or of any level, or the switches in Settings. Haptics can be turned off in Settings too.

Is it colour blind friendly?

Turn on Colour patterns in Settings to add a different texture to each colour area.
