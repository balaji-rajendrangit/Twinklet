Light the stars, one puzzle at a time

Twinklet is a calm logic puzzle for iPhone and Android. Place one star in every row, every column and every colour area, and never let two stars touch. Every puzzle has exactly one answer and never needs a guess.

  • 1,000 levels across 20 worlds, from gentle to expert
  • A new puzzle every night, with streaks
  • Each level lights a star of a real constellation: light all 88
  • Hints that explain the reasoning, not just the answer
  • Music-box night music, sounds and haptics
  • No sign-in needed, plays fully offline
